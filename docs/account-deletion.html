Account Deletion

This page explains how to delete your Click Counter account and the data associated with it. This complies with the Google Play account-deletion requirement.

Delete your account from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Click Counter on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the action. You will be asked to sign in again with your account (Google or email/password) to prove identity.
  4. Once confirmed, your account and data are deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app on your device, send an email to shiddeshwaran@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Click Counter account. Include the subject line “Account Deletion Request”. We will verify ownership and delete your account within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (the email/Google identity used to sign in).
  • Your encrypted cloud backup of Android system / secure / global settings.
  • The escrowed encryption key used to decrypt your backups across devices.
  • Your profile metadata (display name and email stored at sign-up).
  • All local snapshots, cached settings, and the encrypted local database on the device when you trigger deletion in-app.

What is retained, and for how long

Account deletion is immediate — once confirmed, the records above are removed from our servers. We do not retain encrypted backups, encryption keys, or profile metadata after deletion.

Feedback messages you previously submitted through the in-app “Send feedback” form are retained without any link to your account for product-improvement purposes. They contain only the message text and anonymous device metadata (app version, Android API level, device model) that you submitted at the time. If you want a specific feedback record removed, mention it in your deletion email.

Diagnostic data collected by Firebase Crashlytics is anonymised and aggregated; it cannot be tied back to your account after deletion.
